Joie Zhang

PhD Student at MIT CSAIL, Princeton CS '24

Hi 👋! I broadly work on problems related to evaluating language model capabilities, systems programming for machine learning, and AI for science. I co-lead the GPU MODE leaderboard, where we recently hosted two $100k competitions with AMD and a model optimization competition with Jane Street.

I am a PhD student at MIT CSAIL. I spent the summer before at Sakana AI. I spent a year before that as a researcher at VantAI working on AI-based drug discovery.

Before that, I graduated as the top student of the Princeton CS department, where I was blessed with amazing mentors: Professor Karthik Narasimhan, Dr. Khanh Nguyen, Dr. Ofir Press, and Professor Kai Li. During that time, I was doing some blend of AI/ML research while interning at Snapchat, Apple , and Claryo. Before that, I used to make and sell PC games, one of which was mildly successful (~100k+ players). [unfinished example]
